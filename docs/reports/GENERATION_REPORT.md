# Generation report — the portrait app

What the I-Am-Supernatural portrait generator produced during the campaign, reconstructed from the one record the
backend kept: the per-visitor rate-limit table in Supabase (`rate_limits`: IP, day, count, last generation time).
Raw aggregates: [`data/generation.json`](data/generation.json). No IP addresses are published.

> **These numbers are a lower bound.** The app deliberately stored no images, no prompts and no scene id
> (see "Image Storage" in the README). Its table keeps **one row per visitor, holding only their most recent day**, so any
> earlier days a returning visitor generated on are overwritten. Treat every figure below as "at least".

## What was set up around generation

- Per-IP quota: 2 images a day with a 5-minute cooldown, stored in Supabase so it survived Render restarts.
- Service-wide daily caps by weekday and closed days (Wednesday to Friday) to hold a fixed budget; a global counter row in the same table.
- Concurrency regulator: at most 3 simultaneous Gemini calls, queue depth 12.
- Model note: the original model was retired by Google on 15 Jan 2026 and its replacement on 2 Oct 2026, mid-campaign. Details in
  [PROMPT_LESSONS](../PROMPT_LESSONS.md).

## What was not recorded

- Images, prompts, scene ids and custom "twist" text (by design, for privacy and cost).
- Failed generations, and Gemini's own token and billing totals (see your Google AI Studio / Cloud billing console).
- Shares and downloads (they happen in the visitor's browser).
- **The announcement-poster app** ([`minister-poster/`](../../minister-poster/)) saved nothing server-side: its Supabase and Cloudinary
  settings were never filled in, so no count of posters or videos can be reconstructed.
